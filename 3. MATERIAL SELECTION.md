## 3.1 Overview

Material selection directly affects structural strength, fatigue performance, weight, and cost. This section documents the selection rationale for the frame material used throughout this study.

## 3.2 Candidate Materials Considered

| Material | Pros | Cons |
|-----|----|----|
| Plain Carbon Steel	| High strength, good weldability, low cost, widely available	| Higher density (heavier than aluminum) |
| Aluminum Alloy 	| Lightweight, good corrosion resistance	| Lower fatigue strength, more expensive, harder to weld reliably
| Mild Steel	| Very low cost, easy to work with	| Lower yield strength, less predictable fatigue behavior

## 3.3 Selection Rationale

Plain Carbon Steel was selected based on:

Adequate strength-to-weight ratio for a human-carrying structural frame

Good weldability for frame fabrication

Established fatigue data availability for design validation 

Cost-effectiveness given project constraints
