### Calculating Dynamic Center of Gravity :-

Lets consider data from (4. Center of gravity)

1. Human mass = 100kg
2. Segway mass = 65kg
3. Static CG = 1.16m
4. Motor diameter = 10in (0.254m)
5. Wheel diameter = 310mm
6. Track width = 778.61mm
7. Half track = 389.3mm
8. Motor torque total = 50Nm (Each motor = 25Nm)
9. Max speed = 20km/h
10. Turning radius = 1.5m

#### 1. Driving force

$$F = \frac{T}{r} = \frac{50}{0.155} = 322.6N$$

#### 2. Max Acceleration

$$a = \frac{F}{m} = \frac{322.6}{165} = 1.96m/s^2$$

#### 3. Dynamic CG Shift during Acceleration

$$\Delta x =\frac{ha}{g} = \frac{1.16*1.96}{9.81} = 0.232m$$

#### 4. Acceleration

$$ac = \frac{V^2}{R} (Where V = 5.56m/s^2 and R = 1.5m)$$

$$ac = \frac{5.56^2}{1.5} = 20.6m/s^2$$

#### 5. Dynamic Tipping Angle

$$\theta = 18.57 - tan^-1\frac{1.96}{9.81}$$

$$tan^-1(0.2) = 11.30$$

$$\theta = 18.57 - 11.30 = 7.27deg$$
