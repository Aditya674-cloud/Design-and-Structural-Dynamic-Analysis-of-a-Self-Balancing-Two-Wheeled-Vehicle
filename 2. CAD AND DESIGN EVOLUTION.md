## 2.1 Overview

This section documents the mechanical design of the self-balancing vehicle chassis, including its evolution from initial concept to final validated geometry. Design decisions at each stage were driven by structural requirements (Section 08 onward), CG placement targets (Section 04), and manufacturability considerations.

## 2.2 Design Requirements

Before detailing the geometry, the key design drivers were:

**1. Support a rider of ~100 kg with adequate structural margin**

**2. Position the CG to allow stable balancing without excessive control torque demand**

**3. Accommodate hub motor and wheel assembly (10-inch hub motors)**

**4. Minimize unnecessary mass while maintaining structural stiffness (frame natural frequency requirements, Section 11)**
