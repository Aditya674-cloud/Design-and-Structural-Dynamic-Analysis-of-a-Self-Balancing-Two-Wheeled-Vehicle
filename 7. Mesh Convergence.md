# Here the mesh convergence is done on the final optimized design. 

##Method

The static study from Section 06 was re-run at four progressively finer mesh sizes but Element count is kept same, monitoring maximum Von Mises stress at each step.

| Mesh Size (mm) (Max - Min) | Element Count | Max Stress (MPa) | % Change from Previous |
|---|---|---|---|
| 17.16 - 15.44 | 8 | 22.63 | — |
| 15 - 13 | 8 | 12.79 | -43.48 |
| 10 - 9  | 8 | 13.10 | +2.42 |
| 5 - 4  | 8 | 14.71 | +12.29 |

### The formula applied to calculate change in stresses than previous is :-

$$Change = \frac{STRESSn - STRESSn-1}{STRESSn-1} * 100$$
