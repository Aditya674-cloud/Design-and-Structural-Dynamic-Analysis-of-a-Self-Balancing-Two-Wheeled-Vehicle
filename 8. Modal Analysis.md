# Every physical structure has natural frequency same way segway frame also has some frequency isn't it ?

## For identifying natural frequency we should know material we using for manufacturing frame :- Plain carbon steel - IS2062 (Grade E250) and has 

### Natural frequency depends on stiffness and mass 

Formula for finding frequency of material is :-

$$Fn\propto\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}\propto\sqrt{\frac{E}{\rho}}$$

Where E = Youngs modulus

$$\rho$$ = Density 

m = mass

k = stiffness

### Property of plain carbon steel (From Solidworks Material Property)

1. Elastic module = 210 GPa
2. Poissions ratio = 0.28
3. Shear modulus = 79 Gpa
4. Mass density = 7800 $$kg/m^2$$
5. Tensile strength = 399826000 $$N/m^2$$
6. Yield strength = 220594000 $$N/m^2$$
7. Ultimate tensile strength = 410 Mpa

#### For simple vibrating system natural frequency is :-

$$Fn = \frac{1}{2\phi}\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}$$

Where k = stiffness , m = mass

#### For structural member, stiffness depends on Youngs modulus 

$$k\propto E$$

#### Similarly mass depends on density and volume 

$$m\propto\rho$$

#### Therefore the natural frequency is 41.634Hz
